## mascot-knorrig.png (site mascot, top bar)

Derived from IKEA's official product photography for the **KNORRIG soft toy, pig/pink** (IKEA article, e.g. ikea.com product page "knorrig-soft-toy-pig-pink"), a real IKEA product. IKEA's own product pages blocked direct automated fetches, so the source photo was retrieved via a Pinterest-hosted copy of the same official studio shot (white background, standard IKEA product-photo styling), then background-removed and color-decontaminated locally to produce a transparent PNG.

This is IKEA's product photography, not an original photo — it's used here as a small in-app mascot icon for a personal, non-commercial hobby project. **Do not treat this as cleared for commercial use, resale, or anything beyond that context** — swap it for original artwork or a licensed asset before any commercial/public-facing use beyond a personal site.
